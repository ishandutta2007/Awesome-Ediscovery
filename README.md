## 🌐 Market Size & Industry Structure

The global e-discovery market is estimated at **$15.2 Billion in 2026** (projected to reach $22.5 Billion by 2030 at a ~10.2% CAGR), driven by exponential data growth, cross-border privacy regulations, and enterprise adoption of generative AI document review. The sector is **moderately fragmented**, anchored by heavyweight market leaders (such as OpenText and Relativity) while witnessing rapid market share expansion from agile AI-first SaaS disruptors (Everlaw, DISCO, Reveal) and specialized open-source forensic tools.

## 🛠️ Additional Specialized Infrastructure

- **🔍 Search & Indexing Engines**: **[Apache Solr](https://solr.apache.org/)** *(powers FreeEed)*, **[Apache Lucene](https://lucene.apache.org/)** *(powers IPED)*, **[Elasticsearch](https://github.com/elastic/elasticsearch)**.
- **🛡️ Forensic Processing Backends**: **[The Sleuth Kit](https://github.com/sleuthkit/sleuthkit)** *(3.1k+ stars)*, **[Volatility 3](https://github.com/volatilityfoundation/volatility3)** *(4.4k+ stars - RAM Memory Forensics)*, **[Plaso / log2timeline](https://github.com/log2timeline/plaso)** *(2.2k+ stars - Forensic Timeline Extraction)*.
- **⚖️ Human Rights & Legal Case Management**: **[CaseBox](https://github.com/huridocs/casebox)** *(HURIDOCS framework for human rights litigation)*.
